Who Am I

Study's

Hi, I'm Christian Lemmen, a software engineering student based in The Hague. I have always enjoyed working with computers, but my true passion sparked when I started programming for the first time. I graduated from ROC Mondriaan as a Full Stack Software Developer (MBO Level 4), gaining strong foundational experience with JavaScript, React, PHP, and database design. I am now continuing my education at De Haagse Hogeschool for my Bachelor's in Software Engineering, continuously expanding my skills in both software development and embedded systems.

Teacher / Instructor

I am an instructor for vacation coding camps, regular courses, weekend camps, and short 3D workshops, where I teach foundational and advanced concepts in software development, robotics, and engineering. I really enjoy simplifying complex technical concepts for students of various ages. In my classes, we work hands-on with robotics and electronics (Arduino, Raspberry Pi, Micro:bit, Lego WeDo 2.0), visual coding (Scratch), and 3D design (Tinkercad) and 3D printing. I love guiding students through debugging, problem-solving, and creative project building while managing classroom dynamics to ensure a productive learning environment.
